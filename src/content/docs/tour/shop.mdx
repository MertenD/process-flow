## Overview

The **Shop** is your gateway to a world of custom nodes that can enhance your process modeling experience. You can browse, install, and manage custom nodes from this page.

![shop overview](/assets/shop.png)

Once you found a node you like, you can see its details and add or remove it from your workspace. After adding a node to your workspace, it will be available in the **Node Library** for you to use in your process models.

![shop node details](/assets/shop-node-details.png)
