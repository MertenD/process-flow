## Overview

![team dashboard overview](/assets/dashboard.png)

The **Team Dashboard** gives you a bird’s-eye view of your teams. You can:

- **Create New Team**: Spin up a brand-new team with its own color scheme.
- **Your Teams**: See and switch to teams you’ve created.
- **Joined Teams**: Access teams you’ve been invited to.
- **Pending Invitations**: Accept or decline team invites.
